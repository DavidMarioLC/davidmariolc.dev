When an app always calls the most powerful model, it pays 1.8 seconds for answers nobody needed to be brilliant. When it always calls the fast one, the answer lands in 200ms and sometimes it is useless.

## The idea

The client does not make the call: a router does, and it makes it with a number.

<ConceptAnimation id="model-router" />

The latency budget is a field on the request, not a hunch in the programmer's head. That is what makes the decision automatable.
