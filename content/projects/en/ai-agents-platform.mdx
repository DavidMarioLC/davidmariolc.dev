## The problem

Automation tends to end up scattered: one team has a script, another a
spreadsheet, a third a chat bot nobody maintains. Nothing is discoverable, and
the people who would benefit most are the ones least able to use it.

## What I did

I built a platform where each business area gets its own specialized agent. Every
agent shares the same conversational interface, so there is one thing to learn
rather than one per team, and behind it each one runs its own n8n workflows.

Keeping the automation in n8n means the logic stays visible and editable by the
people who own the process, instead of living inside code only I can change.

## Result

Each area has an assistant that speaks its own language, and the workflows behind
them can be adjusted without touching the interface.
