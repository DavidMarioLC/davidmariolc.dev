## The problem

The previous site was slow to load and hard to navigate, and every content change
had to go through a developer. For a dealer network where campaigns and equipment
listings change constantly, that was a bottleneck.

## What I did

I split the site in two. WordPress stayed, but only as a headless CMS the
marketing team already knew how to use. The frontend became a Next.js app that
reads from it and renders everything statically.

That gave the team the editing experience they were used to, and gave visitors a
site that loads in a fraction of the time.

## Result

Pages that used to take seconds now render immediately, and content updates ship
without a deploy.
